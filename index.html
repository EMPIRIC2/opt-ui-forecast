What do these numbers mean?

The percentage is the share of forecast scenarios. We run the forecast many times to capture different ways the next few weeks could unfold. The number is how many of those runs reach an extreme day. 40% means 4 in 10 runs.

"How extreme?" sets the bar. Notable days are uncommon, Severe days are rare, and Extreme days are very rare — each bar comes from this county's own history. Rarer bars naturally show lower percentages.

"Extreme" is set by local history. Each county's threshold comes from its own past 20 years, so an extreme day here means weather that has been uncommon for this place.

The forecast is most reliable this week. Confidence fades the further out we look, which is why later weeks are labeled less certain. Use this as an early heads-up, not a guarantee.
